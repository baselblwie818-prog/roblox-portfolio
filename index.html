War Table: WW2 Strategy

My own strategy game, published on Roblox. Europe in 1939 is a giant 3D map you can walk across. You lead one nation's armies, fleets and air forces in real time while the AI runs the rest. I made all of it myself, from the map pipeline and the unit models to the Luau systems, the UI and the store art.

Gameplay
6 playable nations, supply, terrain and winter, recruiting, 15 research upgrades, AI for every nation
Art
13 unit models made in Blender, painted in each nation's colour. Store art rendered from the game's own assets
